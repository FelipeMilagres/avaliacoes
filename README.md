Subject: Questions about video evidence in Test Manager

Hello LambdaTest team, hope you’re doing well.

We have been using both Device Farm and Test Manager in our daily testing activities, and during execution we noticed a couple of points we’d like to better understand with you.

1. Automatic attachments (screenshots vs videos)

Currently, when we capture screenshots during test execution, they are automatically linked to the test scenario and remain available as evidence at the end. This works really well for us.

However, the same does not happen with videos. Even when the execution is recorded, the video is not automatically attached to the scenario, and we need to manually upload it afterwards.

Given our testing volume, this manual step adds a noticeable operational overhead.

We’d like to understand if there is any way to have videos automatically attached as well, similar to screenshots, or if this is something planned for future improvements.

2. Video upload size limitation

Because of this manual upload flow, we ended up identifying another point: there is currently a 5MB limit for video uploads.

During our evaluation and alignment with the commercial team, this topic was discussed and our understanding at the time was that there wouldn’t be a relevant limitation for this type of evidence. This was actually an important factor in our decision, since our current tool allows up to 25MB and we already face some constraints with that limit.

In addition, in our context this becomes even more critical. A significant part of our evidence needs to be in video format due to regulatory requirements and internal audits. In many cases, screenshots alone are not sufficient to validate the behavior of a flow.

So when we combine the lack of automatic video attachment with the current size limitation, it becomes quite challenging for us to properly use this feature.

Could you please clarify if the 5MB limit is fixed or if there is any possibility to increase it? Also, is there any recommended approach within the platform to better handle video evidence?

Looking forward to your guidance on these points.

Thanks in advance for your support.
